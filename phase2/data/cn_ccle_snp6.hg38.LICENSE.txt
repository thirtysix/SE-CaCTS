cn_ccle_snp6.hg38.tsv.gz

Derived from the Cancer Cell Line Encyclopedia (CCLE) 2019 copy-number segments (Affymetrix SNP6, hg19),
as distributed by cBioPortal in the study `ccle_broad_2019` (file data_cna_hg19.seg). Source publication:
Ghandi M, et al. Next-generation characterization of the Cancer Cell Line Encyclopedia. Nature 569, 503-508 (2019).

Changes: segments for the listed DepMap models were lifted from hg19 to hg38 (UCSC hg19ToHg38 chain), split
into pieces of at most 1 Mb, converted from log2 to linear ratio, and re-centred to a length-weighted median
of 1.0 per line (cnrose/cnrose/cn/ccle.py; phase1/scripts/21_ccle_snp6_validate.py --export-keys).
