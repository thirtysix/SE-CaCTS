SE-CaCTS

Lineage-specific super-enhancers across the cancer cell-line panel

Every H3K27ac experiment called for super-enhancers with a copy-number-aware ROSE reimplementation, reduced to one atlas, and scored for group-specificity with the CaCTS Jensen–Shannon divergence. Significance uses a label-permutation null — the analytic approximation it replaces failed calibration outright (6% false calls on shuffled labels).

Calls: supported at the lineage and primary-disease levels (permutation FDR ≤ 0.10).
Rankings only: at subtype and cell-line level — too few lines per group for a significance call.

Copy-number ablation

Scoring with vs without CN correction, both under the permutation null. Two findings: correction removes a handful of recurrent-amplicon false calls — and, dominantly, it rescues thousands of real, copy-neutral specific SEs that amplicon variance was masking in the null.

removed

Amplicon-driven false calls — every one a named recurrent lineage amplicon

These pass the permutation FDR without CN correction and fail with it — significant only because the locus is amplified. Identified with no prior gene list.

Cross-layer concordance

An independent check that the specific SEs mark real biology: genes sitting next to a group-specific SE are themselves specific to that same group, scored separately on DepMap expression. Compared against the background rate, a group-shuffled control, and by distance.

Concordance decays with SE→gene distance

a local link, not a lineage confound
A lineage-wide batch effect would not care how far the SE is from the gene. The monotone decay — while the shuffled control stays flat — is what distinguishes a local regulatory link from a confound.
